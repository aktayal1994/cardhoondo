We are a young service and may add features that need us to pass some of your details to other companies **at your request**. The kinds of companies we may want to work with, and why, are:

|---|---|
| Lenders and financiers (banks, NBFCs and their authorised agents) | So a lender can contact you about a car loan you ask about |

**This list is a disclosure, not your consent, and it does not give us permission to share anything.** Before we share your personal data with any company for its own purposes, we will:

1. Update this policy and tell you in advance.
2. Ask you for a **fresh, separate consent for each such purpose**, shown when it matters, naming the company or companies, the exact details we would send (for example name, mobile number, city and the car you shortlisted) and why.
3. Leave that consent **unticked by default**, with a clear "No thanks" that **does not affect your recommendation** or any other part of CarDhoondo.
4. Tell you plainly **if we may be paid** by that company for the introduction, and keep that clearly separate from, and after, your recommendation. **A payment never changes which cars we recommend or how we rank them.**
5. Let you **withdraw** that consent at any time. The company that receives your details acts as a separate data fiduciary for them, and we will tell you how to reach it. **If a car maker receives your details with your consent, its own privacy notice governs what it does next, which may include passing them to its dealers; we will say so on the consent screen.** We will not send your details to a dealer ourselves.

Old consents will never be stretched to cover a new purpose or a new kind of recipient.

## 7. Possible future features (not available today)

We may one day offer features that use your **credit information** (for example from a credit bureau) to help you judge affordability or loan options. **We do not do this today and we do not collect your credit history, PAN or date of birth.** If we ever add it:

- it would be **entirely optional and never a condition** of getting your recommendation;
- we would ask for your **explicit, separate consent each time**, telling you which bureau or partner is involved, exactly what is fetched, why, whether it is a "hard" or "soft" enquiry, how long we keep it, and how to withdraw;
- it would be obtained **only through a partner legally authorised to access bureau data**, because credit-bureau rules restrict who may obtain it;
- it would **never be shared with dealers**, never used for advertising, never sent to our AI provider, and deleted on a short schedule (we intend no longer than about six months unless you renew your consent).

This is a possible idea, not a promise that it will launch.
